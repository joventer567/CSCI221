This is a program meant to entertain you with turn-based and text-based gameplay 
where you fight squids on your way to the pause. To play, start by compiling the code
with "make", and then run the code with "./final squid_log.txt(or whatever you want to name the text file)". After beating the game,
squid_log.txt will give you some stats to show how you did.

Once you run the code, after reading a short introduction, you will be prompted to input an action. Type in the action you want to do (case sensitive)
and press enter. Some options (like items) have a second menu where you choose which item to use.

Actions:
Fight: Deal damage to squid
Item: Use an item (all items are one-time use!) in your inventory
Special: Use a special power that costs SP. You start the game with 10 SP, and SP is refilled and increased after every battle.
Joke: You have a 1/10 chance of killing the enemy in one hit.
Guard: Next turn, you will take less damage. Especially effective when the squid is charging up for an attack.
Pass: Pass your turn.
Quit: End the game.

Your goal is to defeat all four squids in the game. After both you and the squid go, you will be shown your health, the 
squid's health, and how many special points (used for specials) you have left.
